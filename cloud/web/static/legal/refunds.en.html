Refunds

Effective from {{В_СИЛА_ОТ}}

The Bulgarian version is the binding one; this translation is for convenience.

1. Within the first 14 days

Within 14 days of buying a pack or of a subscription payment you can withdraw without giving a reason. We refund the amount for the unused credits of that purchase. Example: you bought 500 credits for 5 EUR and spent 100 — we refund 4 EUR.

Credits spent on completed tasks are not refunded: by paying you asked for the service to start immediately, and the model providers have already charged for those tasks.

2. How to ask

Write to {{ИМЕЙЛ}} from your account’s email with the words “I withdraw from the purchase” and its date. No reason is needed.

3. How and when we refund

  • Within 14 days of your request, by the same means you paid (to the same card via Stripe). We charge no fee for the refund.
  • The credits for the refunded amount are removed from your account automatically when the refund is made.
  • If you have spent some of them in the meantime, the balance can go below zero; new tasks start once you top up.

4. After the 14 days

Purchased credits do not expire while the account exists, so after 14 days we do not refund money for them. Exceptions:

  • a task failed through our fault — we return the credits;
  • we shut down the service or your account without any breach on your side — we refund the unused purchased credits;
  • we changed the terms and you do not agree — see section 11 of the Terms.

5. Subscription

You can cancel the subscription at any time by writing to {{ИМЕЙЛ}}; it stops from the next month and no further payment is taken. The rules above (14 days, unused credits) apply to the last payment.

6. Disputed payments

If something is wrong with a payment, please write to us first — it is usually faster than a bank dispute. During a bank dispute the account may be suspended until the case is closed.
