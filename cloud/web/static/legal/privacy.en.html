4. Security

Each task runs in its own isolated container with no internet access and no access to other tasks; the model API keys never enter it. Connections are HTTPS only. Passwords and sessions are stored as hashes.

5. Cookies

We use a single cookie — the session after sign-in. It is strictly necessary for the service and does not need consent. There are no other cookies.

You can also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (cpdp.bg) or the authority in your EU country.

If you ask for deletion, we delete the account, conversations and files within 30 days. Payment records are kept as long as the law requires.
